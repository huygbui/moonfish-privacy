Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2025

This policy describes how Moonfish collects, uses, and protects information when you use our podcast generation application.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our service:

  • Podcast content inputs (topics, length, host preferences, format settings)
  • Images you upload for podcast artwork
  • Generated podcast content
  • Anonymous App Store transaction identifiers for purchase verification
  • Basic usage analytics to improve app performance

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, location data, or any other identifying information. No account creation or sign-in is required.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to generate podcast content, store your creations, and verify app purchases. Each user is identified by a random anonymous identifier that cannot be linked to personal identity.

Data Storage and Retention

Your uploaded content and generated podcasts are stored in cloud storage until you choose to delete them. Currently, content is stored in a way that may be accessible via direct links, though these links are not publicly listed or easily discoverable. You have complete control over your data and can remove it at any time through the app.

Important: Do not upload sensitive or private images, as content may be accessible if someone obtains the direct storage link.

Third-Party Services

We use Google AI services to generate podcast content. Your inputs are processed but not permanently stored by these services. We also use standard cloud hosting infrastructure for secure data storage.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption for data transmission and storage to protect your content.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since we do not collect personally identifying information from any users, our app can be used safely by all ages.

Your Rights

You may access, download, or delete your content at any time directly through the app. You may also contact us with any questions about your data or this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
