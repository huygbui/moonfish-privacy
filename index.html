Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2025

This privacy policy describes how Moonfish ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our podcast generation mobile application.

Privacy-First Approach: Moonfish is designed with privacy in mind. We don't collect any personally identifying information about you and require no account creation or sign-in.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our podcast generation service:

  • Podcast content inputs: Topics, length preferences, host configurations, and format settings you provide
  • Uploaded artwork: Images you upload for your podcast artwork
  • Generated content: The podcasts we create based on your inputs
  • App transaction ID: Anonymous purchase identifiers from the App Store to link your purchases to your content
  • Anonymous usage data: Basic app performance and feature usage (no personal data)

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information: Name, email, phone number, or contact details
  • No location data: We don't track where you are
  • No device access: We don't access your contacts, photos (beyond what you choose to upload), or other personal files
  • No account required: You can use Moonfish without creating an account or signing in

How We Identify Users

Each user is assigned a random, anonymous identifier (UUID) that cannot be linked back to you personally. For purchases, we use the App Store's anonymous transaction ID to connect your paid features to your content without knowing your identity.

How We Use Your Information

  • Generate podcasts: Process your inputs using AI services to create your requested podcast content
  • Store your content: Save your uploaded artwork and generated podcasts so you can access them
  • Manage purchases: Link your App Store purchases to enable paid features
  • Improve our service: Analyze anonymous usage patterns to enhance the app experience

Data Storage and Retention

  • Your content: Uploaded artwork and generated podcasts are stored securely on our servers
  • Retention period: Content is stored until you choose to delete it
  • Deletion: You can delete your content at any time through the app
  • Complete control: We only keep your content as long as you want us to

Third-Party Services

Moonfish uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • App Store: For app distribution, updates, and purchase processing
  • Google AI Services: For podcast content generation (your inputs are processed but not permanently stored by Google)
  • Cloud hosting services: For secure content storage and app infrastructure

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your uploaded content and generated podcasts. All data is transmitted securely using encryption and stored using secure cloud infrastructure.

Children's Privacy

Moonfish does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we don't collect personally identifying information from any users, children can safely use our app under parental guidance.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your content: View all podcasts and artwork you've created
  • Delete your content: Remove any uploaded artwork or generated podcasts at any time
  • Data portability: Download your generated podcasts in standard formats
  • Complete control: Manage all your data directly through the app
  • Contact us: Ask questions about your data or this policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
