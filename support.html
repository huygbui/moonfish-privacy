Podcast Generation Problems

  • If podcast generation fails, check your internet connection and try again
  • Ensure your topic input is clear and specific for better results
  • Try shorter topics if you experience timeouts
  • Restart the app if generation seems stuck

Audio Playback Issues

  • Make sure your device volume is turned up
  • Check that your device isn't in silent mode
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • Restart your device if audio problems persist

Purchase and Subscription Issues

  • App Store purchases are handled by Apple - check your App Store receipt
  • To restore purchases, use the "Restore Purchases" option in app settings
  • For refunds, contact Apple Support directly through the App Store

Getting Better Results

To create the best possible podcasts with Moonfish:

  • Be specific and descriptive in your topic inputs
  • Choose appropriate length settings for your content
  • Upload high-quality images for better artwork
  • Experiment with different host voice combinations

Data and Content

All your generated podcasts are saved in the app and can be accessed anytime. To delete content, use the delete option within each podcast. If you need help accessing or managing your content, please contact us.

Technical Requirements

Moonfish requires an internet connection to generate podcasts. Make sure you have a stable connection when creating content. The app works best on iOS devices running the latest system software.
